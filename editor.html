design your own pet · strays

Paint two walk frames (or drop in a PNG — transparency works best), name it, adopt it. It joins the team on the demo page and in the desktop overlay. Format is a tiny JSON of pixel grids — this whole thing is open source, PRs with new pets welcome.

CANVAS — walk frame 1

left-drag paints · right-drag erases · '1/2/3' are the light→dark body tones — a Crew-style outline is added automatically. 'k'+'w' make an eye (black square, white glint), 'p' is pink for noses and tongues.
